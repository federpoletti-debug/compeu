IV · How it works

Old rules of measure, for a new commodity.

In the 1790s France defined the metre and the kilogram, so that anyone could check a measure. GPU time has no such standard yet. CompEU builds one in the open, in three steps that repeat every morning.

  1. I Collect the public pricesA robot reads the price lists of {{facts.collectors}} clouds, from their public APIs and price pages: {{facts.offers}} prices on the last run, {{facts.lastRun}}. No estimates, no prices by e-mail.
  2. II Keep only the sovereign cloudsEach provider faces one rule: a legal seat in the EU or Switzerland, control from Europe, and no foreign parent that a court abroad can compel. Of {{facts.providers}} clouds, {{facts.nSov}} pass, {{facts.nRev}} are under review and {{facts.nNon}} fail.
  3. III Fix the median, seal itFor each GPU, the median price becomes the day’s fixing in euro, at the European Central Bank rate. It is sealed with a hash chained to every fixing before it, so the past cannot be rewritten quietly.
