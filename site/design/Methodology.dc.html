IV · Limits

What the index does not claim.

List pricesPublished on-demand prices, not negotiated contracts. Large buyers pay less, at both kinds of provider.
Catalogue pricesSome providers publish one European price list without sites. Such a list counts once, with the reason written in the registry.
Provisional facts{{nProv}} providers have ownership facts still being confirmed. They are marked wherever they appear.
Not a regulated benchmarkCompEU is information. It is not offered as a benchmark for financial contracts, and it is not investment advice.
