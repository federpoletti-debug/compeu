II · Reach

A server in Frankfurt can still answer to Washington.

The US CLOUD Act of 2018 obliges US providers to disclose the data they control “regardless of whether such communication, record, or other information is located within or outside of the United States”. What decides is who owns the provider, not where the server stands.
