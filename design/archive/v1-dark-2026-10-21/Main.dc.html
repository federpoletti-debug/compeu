Rule sovereignty/1 · in force since 23 Sep 2026

Control, not location

The US CLOUD Act lets US authorities require a US-controlled provider to hand over data wherever it is stored. So the test looks at the company, the way France's SecNumCloud and the EU's draft cloud certification do. A provider is sovereign only if all three answers are yes.

1 · Seat Registered in the EU or Switzerland

The contracting company's legal seat is in one of the EU's 27 member states or in Switzerland.

2 · Control Owned and controlled there too

The ultimate owner is also European. A German subsidiary of a US group fails here.

3 · Reach Out of reach of the CLOUD Act

No parent subject to the US CLOUD Act, or to a similar law with access to data abroad.
